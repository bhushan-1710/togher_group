> This document set is intended to guide a production-quality redesign. Verified company facts are separated from design proposals. Any fact that could not be verified is explicitly marked `[VERIFY]`.

## SEO Strategy
Target intent around construction recruitment, construction engineering recruitment, trades and labour recruitment, and relevant geographic markets only where supported by current services. Avoid keyword stuffing.

## Proposed Page Titles
- Home: `Togher Group | Construction & Engineering Recruitment`
- About: `About Togher Group | Construction Recruitment Specialists`
- Management Professionals: `Management Professionals Recruitment | Togher Group`
- Trades & Labour: `Trades & Labour Recruitment | Togher Group`
- Clients: `Construction Recruitment for Clients | Togher Group`
- Candidates: `Construction Careers & Recruitment | Togher Group`
- Contact: `Contact Togher Group | Construction Recruitment`

Final titles require approval and current keyword validation.

## H1
One H1 per page. Match user intent, not internal navigation labels.

## Structured Data
Consider Organization, LocalBusiness where legally/semantically appropriate, BreadcrumbList, WebSite and JobPosting only for real current vacancies.

## Canonicals
Every indexable page gets a self-referencing canonical unless a deliberate canonical strategy is required.

## Open Graph
Use a consistent branded 1200x630 image with page title/descriptor.

## Images
Descriptive alt text; empty alt for decorative images.

## Sitemap / Robots
Generate automatically from the final route set. Exclude utility/thank-you pages where appropriate.
