# Togher Group Website Redesign

**Project:** Premium website redesign / demo
**Client:** Togher Group
**Primary source:** https://www.toghergroup.com/
**Status:** Documentation / pre-development
**Source of truth:** `SOURCE_OF_TRUTH.md`

> This document set is intended to guide a production-quality redesign. Verified company facts are separated from design proposals. Any fact that could not be verified is explicitly marked `[VERIFY]`.

## 1. Product Overview
Togher Group needs a digital experience that communicates its construction-sector recruitment and related services with the confidence of an established international organisation. The redesign should make the business model immediately understandable to clients, candidates and partners while creating a premium, editorial visual experience.

## 2. Verified Client Context
The current website describes Togher Group as an international construction recruitment consultancy serving Ireland, the UK, Europe, USA and Australia. ## 3. Business Objectives
- Strengthen perceived credibility and maturity of the group.
- Clarify the relationship between recruitment, trades/labour and consultancy.
- Improve client enquiry and candidate pathways.
- Present geographic reach without resorting to unsupported claims.
- Build a platform that can accommodate vacancies, insights and future project/service content.

## 4. Website Objectives
1. Explain who Togher is within seconds.
2. Make service discovery intuitive.
3. Give clients a direct path to discuss staffing requirements.
4. Give candidates a direct path to vacancies/CV submission.
5. Establish credibility through people, sectors, process and verified experience.
6. Make the site feel premium without looking like a generic corporate template.

## 5. Audiences
### Primary
- Construction and engineering clients seeking management professionals.
- Construction and engineering clients seeking trades and labour.
- Candidates seeking permanent or temporary roles.

### Secondary
- Contractors, project partners and industry stakeholders.
- People researching Togher before making contact.
- Prospective employees.

## 6. User Needs
- Understand the service quickly.
- Know whether Togher operates in their market.
- Understand how the recruitment process works.
- See an obvious next action.
- Find contact details without friction.
- Trust the organisation before submitting personal information.

## 7. Core Value Proposition
**Working proposition:** Construction-sector expertise connected to recruitment, workforce solutions and international opportunities. Final proposition must be validated against client-approved positioning.

## 8. Conversion Architecture
**Primary CTA:** `Work With Togher` / `Discuss Your Requirement` — select after stakeholder validation.

**Secondary CTAs:**
- Explore Services
- Find Opportunities / View Vacancies
- About Togher
- Contact Togher

## 9. Functional Requirements
### MUST HAVE
- Responsive navigation and mobile menu.
- Service pages.
- Client enquiry/contact form.
- Candidate application/CV submission path if retained from current functionality.
- Vacancy path if current vacancy data is available.
- Accessible form validation.
- SEO metadata and structured headings.
- Optimised responsive images.

### SHOULD HAVE
- Project/experience or sector storytelling where verified content exists.
- Insights/news architecture.
- Reusable CMS-ready content structures.
- Analytics event tracking.

### NICE TO HAVE
- Advanced filtering for opportunities.
- Editorial case-study templates.
- Lightweight interactive global reach map.

## 10. Required Pages
Recommended baseline: Home, About / Group, Services, Management Professionals, Trades & Labour, Careers / Candidates, Clients / Recruitment, Vacancies, Insights, Contact. Add Projects/Sectors only when supported by verified content.

## 11. Non-Functional Requirements
- Fast first load and responsive interaction.
- No layout shift caused by images/fonts.
- Keyboard navigable.
- Reduced-motion support.
- Clear error and success states.
- No fabricated claims, statistics, testimonials, project names or credentials.

## 12. Success Criteria
- Users can identify business purpose from the first viewport.
- Users can reach a relevant service in one or two interactions.
- Client and candidate conversion routes are distinct.
- Mobile experience is intentionally designed.
- Visual system remains premium without decorative excess.
- All factual content is traceable to approved sources.

## 13. Risks
- Existing site content may be outdated or inconsistent across regional pages.
- Brand assets may not be available in production-ready formats.
- Vacancies, team data, testimonials and project imagery may require client supply.
- International claims need careful wording and current verification.

## 14. Assumptions
- This is a redesign/demo and not an instruction to alter legal/company facts.
- Existing contact details should only be reused after client confirmation.
- Current recruitment application functionality is a reference for required flows, not necessarily the final implementation.
