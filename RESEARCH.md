## 2. Existing Website Observations
- Service and recruitment content is available but currently presented in a relatively conventional corporate/recruitment structure.
- Candidate flow is functional and form-driven.
- There is an opportunity to improve narrative hierarchy, visual credibility and separation of user journeys.
These are design observations, not claims about business performance.

## 6. UX Conclusions
- Put business positioning first.
- Give clients and candidates distinct pathways.
- Use services as the primary information architecture.
- Use people and verified experience as credibility layers.
- Make enquiry actions easy without making the site feel sales-heavy.

## 7. Technical Considerations
- Next.js/TypeScript/Tailwind provides a suitable component-driven foundation.
- Image optimisation is critical because the design depends heavily on photography.
- Motion should remain lightweight and accessible.
